What is MindSpeak?

MindSpeak is an application designed to help people with ASD who have difficulty expressing their emotions. Our platform offers an intuitive and visual way to communicate feelings, allowing users to select from different emotions with various intensity levels and add a description of why they feel that way.

Additionally, the application provides valuable tools for supervisors and family members, who can receive notifications when the user records an emotion, check the emotional history, and visualize trend graphs over time. They can also assign personalized exercises and resources to help manage emotions.

Developed by students of the Higher Education Cycle in Multiplatform Application Development, MindSpeak was born from the desire to use technology to improve the quality of life of people with ASD, facilitating emotional communication in a comfortable and safe way.
