Marketing Strategy

How we plan to promote MindSpeak and generate sustainable revenue

Revenue Model

Free Individual Version

  • Basic features for individual users
  • Limit of 2 assigned users
  • History limited to 30 days

Family Subscription

€4.99/month

  • Up to 5 assigned users
  • Unlimited complete history
  • Advanced graphs and statistics
  • Unlimited personalized exercises

License for Centers

€29.99/month

  • Up to 30 assigned users
  • All Premium features
  • Multi-user administration console
  • Priority technical support
  • Application customization
