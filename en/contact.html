Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about MindSpeak

What is MindSpeak?

MindSpeak is a mobile application designed to help people with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) express their emotions in an easy and visual way. The app also provides tools for supervisors and family members to monitor users' emotional states.

How can I download MindSpeak?

Currently, MindSpeak is available as a prototype for Android devices. You can download it from our home page or by clicking here. The iOS version is under development and will be available soon.

Is the application free?

MindSpeak offers a basic free version for individual users with limited features. We also have subscription plans for families and licenses for educational and therapeutic centers that offer advanced features. You can find more details in our Business Model section.

Which devices are compatible with MindSpeak?

Currently, MindSpeak is compatible with Android devices running version 8.0 or higher. We are working on the iOS version and a web version that will be available in the coming months.

How are user data protected?

At MindSpeak, we take privacy and data security very seriously. We use end-to-end encryption for all sensitive data, and we comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You can find more information in our Privacy Policy.

Can I collaborate with the MindSpeak project?

We are open to collaborations with professionals, educational centers, associations, and other entities interested in improving emotional communication for people with ASD. If you would like to collaborate, you can contact us through the form on this page or by sending an email to collaborations@mindspeak-app.com.
