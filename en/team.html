Who We Are

We are students of the Higher Education Program in Multiplatform Application Development and, thanks to this training, we've discovered that programming and databases are fields that we're passionate about.

Additionally, we have a great interest in putting our knowledge and skills at the service of others, helping as many people as possible. With this goal in mind, we decided to design and develop an application that could be useful and practical.

To carry out this project, we leveraged our diverse capabilities and organized ourselves so that each member could contribute in different areas, assigning specific roles according to our strengths. In this way, together, we've been able to work collaboratively to bring this idea to reality.

Working Methodology

How we worked to develop MindSpeak

SCRUM Methodology

For the development of this project, we used the SCRUM methodology, which allowed us to work in an agile and collaborative way. This methodology was based on:

  • Daily or weekly meetings: Where we shared the status of tasks completed by each member and distributed new tasks, planning what we would do until the next meeting.
  • Organization with Trello: We used this program to assign tasks in a visual and intuitive way, facilitating coordination and tracking of each person's progress.
  • Sprints: We divided the development into four main sprints, each with specific objectives.
  • Version control with GitHub: We used GitHub to maintain version control of the code, allowing multiple members to work in parallel without conflicts.

This combination of meetings and the use of tools like Trello and GitHub helped keep the work well-organized, transparent, and with fluid communication between all team members.

Our Learning Experience

What we've learned during the development of MindSpeak

Teamwork

We've learned the importance of good organization and communication in a development team. The assignment of specific roles has allowed us to leverage the strengths of each member, thus optimizing the work process.

Accessible Design

The project has taught us to design with accessibility and ease of use in mind, ensuring that the application is truly useful and functional for users with ASD. We've considered aspects such as colors, text size, and visual simplicity.

Technical Solutions

We've learned to adapt to unforeseen circumstances and to find creative solutions to technical challenges, such as the need to use two different databases to resolve limitations with image storage.

Social Impact

This project has shown us that, with effort and collaboration, we can create technological tools that have a positive impact on society, especially for groups with specific needs.
