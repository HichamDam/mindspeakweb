Use Cases

Scenarios where MindSpeak can make a difference

School Environment

MindSpeak helps students with ASD communicate their emotional state to teachers and support staff, facilitating better integration and monitoring during the school day.

"The application has allowed Alex to communicate when he feels anxious in class, enabling us to anticipate and prevent stressful situations."
— Maria Puig, Special Education Teacher

Home Environment

At home, MindSpeak facilitates emotional communication between parents and children, helping to identify triggers for negative emotions and reinforce positive moments.

"For the first time, our son can express when he feels overstimulated without having to go through a crisis."
— Roig Martí Family

Therapy Sessions

Therapists use MindSpeak as a monitoring tool between sessions, allowing them to analyze emotional trends and adapt interventions according to specific needs.

"The emotional history provides me with valuable information to prepare sessions and evaluate progress."
— Dr. Ferran Costa, Psychologist Specializing in ASD

Social Environments

In social situations, MindSpeak offers a discreet way to express emotions when verbal communication is difficult, facilitating interaction with friends and acquaintances.

"Now Julia can participate in group activities with more confidence because she can communicate how she feels at all times."
— Montse Vila, Activities Coordinator
